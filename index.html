NFT Mining on Ethereum

The new home for every NFT on Ethereum. Fair distribution of a new NFC token to real holders, with the collections that give holders creative rights mining hardest.

01
Your NFTs are the miners
Enroll from any wallet, including cold wallets through delegate.xyz or warm.xyz. No staking, no lock-up, nothing leaves your wallet.
02
Hashrate from the market
Each collection's hashrate is the cube root of its reference bid, set 20% under the best external bid. Bigger, more liquid collections mine more, but never proportionally.
03
Rights-weighted
Collections that grant holders CC0 or commercial rights get the full multiplier. Personal-use and restricted collections still mine, at a lower tier.
04
Bitcoin schedule
50 NFC per 20-minute block, halving every 210,000 blocks, 21M cap. Genesis airdrop to today's Pentagon Chain NFC holders. No team allocation.
05
Dual mining
Pentagon Chain keeps running. ETH miners also earn PC for gas, so one wallet can mine both networks.

NFT Mining · how it works

Your NFTs are the miners.

Proof of stake for NFT holders. Hold blue-chip NFTs, enrol them, and every 20 minutes one wallet wins the block. Nothing is deposited, locked or sold.

1
Hold, don't stake. Your NFTs stay in your wallet. Enrolling is a signature. Cold wallets can mine through a delegated hot wallet.
2
One block every 20 minutes. A winner is drawn, weighted by hashrate. The winner takes the whole 50 NFC reward, like a Bitcoin block. Halving every 210,000 blocks, 21 million cap.
3
Hashrate comes from value, not hardware. In Bitcoin you buy machines and the biggest buyer wins. Here hashrate is the cube root of what your NFT is worth, so an NFT worth 1,000× more mines only 10× harder. Nobody can buy up the network.
4
Creators' rights weigh more. Collections that give holders the rights to their own art mine at full power. Personal-use-only collections still mine, just less.
